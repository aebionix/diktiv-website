A few quick answers.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for dictation with the included German model. Additional speech recognition models can be downloaded when needed. Installing Diktiv from the Microsoft Store also requires an internet connection.

Where can I insert my text?

In the input field you are working in, for example in Word, Outlook, a browser or a terminal. You can adjust the insertion method for applications with particular requirements.

Which languages are available?

The interface is available in German, French, Italian and English. The languages you can dictate in depend on the Whisper model you choose.

Is the recognised text always correct?

Speech recognition makes mistakes. Review the text before using it. Diktiv is not a medical device.

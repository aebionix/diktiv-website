Speech to text, right on your device.

Diktiv is a dictation app for Windows. You hold a key, speak and let go. The text appears where your cursor is.

Speech recognition runs entirely on your computer. Recordings and dictations never leave your device. There is no account and no tracking.

Diktiv is coming soon to the Microsoft Store.
